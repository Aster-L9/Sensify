# Sensify held-out set: card

Independent evaluation set (design 6.2), generated by `backend/ml/generate_heldout.py` from the simulator (`app.sim`) without any use of the analysis or investigation code. Labels come only from scenario settings and from the observable events of each simulated run. Labels are not changed after the first evaluation look (design 6.6).

Regenerate from `backend/`: `python -m ml.generate_heldout` (seed 4242). Files: `runs.csv`, `telemetry.csv.gz`, `events.csv`, `ground_truth.csv` (same format as `ml/data`, every `split` is `heldout`) and `labels.csv`.

## Settings (all outside the development range)

| Setting | Held-out | Development |
|---|---|---|
| Strength | 0.1 to 0.3 (small leaks 0.1 to 0.2) | 0.3 to 1.0 |
| Onset | 1, 2, 3, 4, 10, 12, 14, 16 or 18 min | 4 to 10 min |
| Ramp | 2 to 5 min (4 to 5 for slow drift) | 1 min |
| Active phase | 9 to 15 min | 4 to 8 min |
| Run length | 25 to 30 min | 20 min |
| Recovery | off in every odd-indexed slot (30 of 60 scenario runs) | on in 80% |

## Counts

72 runs: 12 each of normal, physical_anomaly, sensor_fault, telemetry_manipulation, unauthorized_control and concealed_physical_incident. Normal runs last 25 to 30 min.

## Ambiguity recipes (20 ambiguous runs)

Seeds are searched until the observable events satisfy the recipe.

| Recipe | Runs | Condition | Acceptable outcomes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| False offset, no logged change | 4 | method bias, no "Reporting configuration change" event | cyber_manipulation, sensor_fault, uncertain |
| Slow sensor drift | 4 | method drift, ramp 4 to 5 min | sensor_fault, uncertain |
| Small leak at an end segment | 4 | leak at SEG-01 or SEG-06, strength 0.1 to 0.2 | physical_anomaly, uncertain |
| Unauthorized control, no alert | 4 | no "Command from unregistered workstation" event | cyber_manipulation, uncertain |
| Overlap with operator change | 4 (one each: physical_anomaly, sensor_fault, unauthorized_control, concealed_physical_incident) | an authorized operator command within 1 min (12 ticks) of onset | expected category, normal_behaviour, uncertain |

Non-ambiguous telemetry-manipulation runs always have a "Reporting configuration change" event, and non-ambiguous unauthorized-control runs always have the unregistered-workstation event.

## Labels

`labels.csv`: run_id, ambiguous, expected_category, acceptable_outcomes (`|` separated), expected_segment (target; blank for normal), expected_ot_layers (`|` separated; blank for normal), ambiguity_note. Category and layers follow the design table (telemetry_manipulation: cyber_manipulation/reporting; unauthorized_control: cyber_manipulation/control; concealed_physical_incident: reporting_inconsistency/physical_process|reporting).
